Research Work

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the SAO (Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory) based on Hilo, Hawaii. My research focuses on forming and evolved stars.

I graduated from the University of Maryland at College Park. During my graduate studies, I worked with Drs. Lee Mundy, James Stone, and Eve Ostriker on molecular outflows associated with forming stars. We confronted the Jet and Wind Models with BIMA observations and Hydrodynamical Simulations of Jet- and Wind-driven Protostellar Outflows.

My previous Postdoctoral position was hosted in NASA/JPL granted by NRC. I worked with Dr. Sahai Raghvandra on Shaping of Planetary and Proto-Protoplanetary Nebulae.
